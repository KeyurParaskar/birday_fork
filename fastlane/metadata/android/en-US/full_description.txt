<h2><b>Introduction 👋</b></h2>
Auspice remembers birthdays and events for you, and adds something no other reminder does: what the world's traditions say about each person and each birthday. Western and Chinese astrology, numerology, Mayan day signs, birthstones, weekday lore, other religious calendars, and the lucky and unlucky ages celebrated around the world.

Everything is computed on your device. No account, no network, no ads, no tracking.

Auspice is built on Birday, the open source birthday manager by m.i.n.a.r., and is free software under the GPL-3 licence.

<h2><b>Reminders 🎂</b></h2>
<ul>
    <li> Simple events timeline, with the next date and the name of each person </li>
    <li> Import birthdays from contacts or calendar, with duplicate detection </li>
    <li> Customizable notification the day of the event, plus advance notifications </li>
    <li> Backup your events in native, csv or json format </li>
    <li> Light, dark and black AMOLED themes, Material 3 Expressive, Monet accents </li>
    <li> Two widgets, a yearly overview, search, favorites and statistics </li>
    <li> 30+ languages </li>
</ul>

<h2><b>Traditions ✨</b></h2>
<ul>
    <li> Western sun sign with element, modality and ruling planet </li>
    <li> Chinese animal, element and yin/yang, using the real lunar new year </li>
    <li> Numerology: Life Path, Birthday, Expression, Soul Urge and Personality numbers </li>
    <li> Mayan Tzolk'in day sign </li>
    <li> Birthstones and birth flowers </li>
    <li> Weekday lore from England, Thailand and Ghana </li>
    <li> Islamic calendar birth date </li>
    <li> Milestone years: Ben Ming Nian, yakudoshi, kanreki, hwangap, golden birthdays and more </li>
    <li> Every tradition can be switched off individually </li>
</ul>

Traditions are presented as cultural trivia with their origin, never as advice or prediction.

Please note: the notification system relies on the Android OS resources, thus some manufacturers may cause malfunctions, because some devices kill apps instead of simply closing them. You can find more information on dontkillmyapp.com.
